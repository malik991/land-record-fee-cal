<h2 className='font-semibold mb-1'>🌿 What is Green Property Certificate (GPC)?</h2>

The **Green Property Certificate (GPC)** is an official document issued by the Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) that verifies the **ownership, possession, and legal status** of a property.

It is especially important in modern property dealings as it provides **verified and transparent land information**, reducing fraud and disputes.

<h2 className='font-semibold my-1'>⭐ Why GPC is Important?</h2>

- Ensures **verified ownership details**
- Confirms **actual possession of land**
- Helps in **safe property transactions**
- Reduces chances of **fraud and litigation**
- Useful for **banks, buyers, and investors**

<h3 className='font-bold text-sm font-base text-red-600 my-1'> ⚠️Important Update (Effective July 2026):</h3>  
> The Government of Punjab has made the **Green Property Certificate (GPC)** mandatory for property transactions.  
> Landowners will be required to obtain a GPC before initiating sale, transfer, or mutation processes.  
> GPC is being introduced as a **primary verified document** for land transactions to ensure transparency and authenticity.

<h2 className='font-semibold my-1'> 👤 Eligibility Criteria</h2>

Eligibility for **Green Property Certificate (GPC)** depends on the ownership structure of the land:

<h3 className='font-bold text-sm font-base text-red-600 my-1'> ✅ Single Ownership (Separate Khewat)</h3>
<ul className="list-disc ml-6">
  <li>If the applicant is the <strong>sole owner</strong> of a Khewat/Khasra</li>
  <li>No consent or additional verification is required</li>
  <li>Applicant can directly apply for GPC</li>
</ul>

<h3 className='font-bold text-sm font-base text-red-600 my-1'> ⚠️ Joint Ownership (Agricultural Land)</h3>
<ul className="list-disc ml-6">
  <li>If the land is <strong>jointly owned (مشترکہ کھاتہ)</strong> and <strong>agricultural</strong></li>
  <li>All co-sharers (shareholders) may be required to:</li>
  <ul className="list-disc ml-10">
    <li>Visit the Arazi Record Center (ARC)</li>
    <li>Confirm and witness the possession of the applicant</li>
  </ul>
</ul>

<h3 className='font-bold text-sm font-base text-red-600 my-1'> 🏙️ Joint Ownership (Urban / Commercial Land)</h3>
<ul className="list-disc ml-6">
  <li>In case of **urban or commercial property**</li>
  <li>Applicant can generally apply for GPC even if ownership is joint</li>
  <li>Physical possession and demarcation may be considered sufficient</li>
</ul>

<h3 className='font-bold text-sm font-base text-red-600 my-1'> 💡 Recommendation</h3>
<ul className="list-disc ml-6">
  <li>It is strongly recommended to:</li>
  <ul className="list-disc ml-10">
    <li>**Partition (تقسیم) joint land holdings**</li>
    <li>Separate your ownership into an independent Khewat/Khasra</li>
  </ul>
</ul>

This helps to:
- Avoid future disputes with co-sharers  
- Simplify GPC issuance and property transactions  
- Ensure smooth verification and transfer process

## 📝 Step-by-Step Procedure

<div>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 1 – Visit Arazi Record Center (ARC)</h3>
<p>👉 Applicant visits the nearest Arazi Record Center (ARC).</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 2 – Submit Application Request</h3>
<p>👉 Applicant provides original CNIC and property details such as Khewat, Khasra, or parcel information to the operator.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 3 – Application Entry & Challan Generation</h3>
<p>👉 ARC operator enters the request into the system and generates a challan (PSID).</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 4 – Deposit Fee</h3>
<p>👉 Applicant deposits the prescribed fee through bank or approved digital payment channels.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 5 – Verification of Payment</h3>
<p>👉 After fee verification, the GPC application is formally processed in the system.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 6 – Forwarding to Surveyor</h3>
<p>👉 The application is forwarded to the concerned <strong>Surveyor</strong> for field verification.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 7 – Land Survey & Possession Verification</h3>
<p>👉 Surveyor visits the site and verifies:</p>

<ul className="list-disc ml-6">
  <li>Land location and boundaries</li>
  <li>Physical possession of land</li>
  <li>Khewat/Khasra details</li>
  <li>Ownership and ground realities</li>
</ul>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 8 – Survey Report Submission</h3>
<p>👉 Surveyor uploads and submits the inspection report into the system.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 9 – Verification by Revenue Officer</h3>
<p>👉 The application and survey report are reviewed by the concerned <strong>Circle Revenue Officer (CRO)</strong> or another authorized gazetted officer.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 10 – GIS Manager Review</h3>
<p>👉 After revenue verification, the case is forwarded to the <strong>GIS Manager</strong> for technical and mapping validation.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 11 – Approval by ADLR</h3>
<p>👉 The complete record is sent to the <strong>Assistant Director Land Records (ADLR)</strong>, who reviews the entire case and grants final approval.</p>

<h3 className='font-semibold'>Step 12 – Issuance of GPC</h3>
<p>👉 After final approval, the Green Property Certificate (GPC) is issued to the applicant.</p>

</div>

12. منظوری کے بعد گرین پراپرٹی سرٹیفکیٹ (GPC) درخواست دہندہ کو جاری کر دیا جائے گا۔
